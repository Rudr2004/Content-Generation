✅ UNIVERSAL SEO & CONTENT GUIDELINE FOR HIRE PAGES
(Applicable to all “Hire [X] Developers” pages)
🔷 1. META INFORMATION
Element	Format
URL Slug	/hire-[developer-type]-developers
Title Tag	`Hire [Developer Type] Developers
Meta Description	Hire top [Developer Type] developers skilled in [Core Technologies]. Fast onboarding. Trusted by global teams in USA & Canada.
➡️ Use localized terms like “USA”, “Canada”, or specific cities in meta when possible.
🔷 2. PAGE HEADINGS (H1-H3)
Level	Usage
H1	Hire [Developer Type] Developers in USA & Canada – On-Demand [Skill/Domain] Experts
H2 Examples	Why Hire From GreenAppleX, Key Highlights, Services We Offer, Our Hiring Models, Testimonials, FAQs
H3 Examples	Used within H2s for subsections like “Smart Contract Development”, “Dedicated Teams”, etc.
🔷 3. HERO SECTION
Goal: Introduce the offering, build trust, include CTA
Elements:
H1: Main keyword
Subheadline: Pain-point solving line or credibility hook
CTA: “Hire [Developer Type]”, “Get Started”, etc.
Trust Badges: Certifications, Awards, Year badges
🔷 4. VALUE & TRUST SECTION ("Why Hire From GreenAppleX")
Format: Short paragraph + 4–6 bullet points
Include:
2x faster hiring
Pre-vetted developers
Time zone aligned
Risk-free trial
Cost savings
NDA-backed teams
🔷 5. METRICS + SOCIAL PROOF BLOCK
Metric	Example
Projects Delivered	100+ [Developer Type] Projects
Years of Experience	8–10+ Years
Revenue / TVL / Installs	$50M+ Secured / 5M+ Installs
Industry Recognition	Top [Tech/Domain] Company 2024
Include logos, client industries, and award badges here.
🔷 6. SERVICES / EXPERTISE OFFERED
Use H2: Our [Developer Type] Services or What Our [Experts] Can Build For You
Use H3 for individual services:
Smart Contract Development
DeFi / AI / Mobile App / SaaS Product Development
Platform Integration / Custom APIs
Security Audits / Testing
Token/NFT/Crypto Development
AI Model Integration / Data Engineering (modify per role)
✅ Add internal links to related service pages or blogs

✅ Use 50–80 words per service with keywords naturally placed
🔷 7. HIRING MODELS SECTION
H2: Flexible Hiring Models for [Developer Type]
Subsections (H3s):
Contract & Contract-to-Hire
Permanent Staffing
Dedicated Teams
For each model, include:
What it is
Ideal use-case
Benefits (speed, cost, team extension, etc.)
🔷 8. TESTIMONIALS & SUCCESS STORIES
Add 2–3 client quotes (name, title, location)
Focus on outcome-based stories
Optional: case study snippets, client logos, star ratings
✅ Example H2: Trusted by Web3 CTOs and Global Founders

✅ Example H3: Client’s name + Company
🔷 9. FAQ SECTION
H2: Frequently Asked Questions
Include 4–6 SEO-enriched questions like:
How to hire [developer type] in [location]?
What is the cost to hire [developer type]?
Are your developers available for dedicated team models?
What technologies do your [developer type] experts specialize in?
✅ Add FAQ Schema for better SERP visibility
🔷 10. CTA / FINAL ENGAGEMENT SECTION
H2: Ready to Hire [Developer Type] Experts?
Include:
Project intake form or lead magnet
Location-specific CTA
“Hire Web3 Developers in New York Today”
Secondary CTA: “Book a Free Consultation”
🔷 11. ON-PAGE SEO ESSENTIALS (FOR DEVELOPERS PAGES)
Element	Practice

Alt Text	Add alt tags to all visuals with developer type + action (e.g., “React developer coding dashboard”)
Internal Links	Link to other hire pages, contact page, blog
Schema Markup	Local Business + Service + FAQ
